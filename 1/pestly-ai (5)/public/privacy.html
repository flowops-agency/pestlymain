Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website or use our services, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact Data: Name, email address, phone number, and any information you voluntarily provide through our contact or demo request forms.
  • Communication Data: Audio recordings and transcriptions generated when you interact with our AI voice assistant demo.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, access timestamps, and device information automatically collected when you visit our website.
  • Security Verification Data: Interaction data processed by Cloudflare Turnstile to distinguish human users from automated bots.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our website and services.
  • To respond to your inquiries, demo requests, and provide customer support.
  • To process and fulfill the AI voice assistant demo functionality you have requested.
  • To protect our website and services against misuse, fraud, and automated attacks.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

The legal basis for processing is your consent (where you have actively opted in), the performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures, our legitimate interest in operating and securing our service, or compliance with legal obligations, as applicable under the GDPR and other relevant data protection laws.

3. AI Voice Assistant (Retell AI)

Our voice AI demo is powered by Retell AI (Retell Inc., USA). When you initiate a demo call, your voice communication is processed in real time through Retell AI servers to generate automated responses. The following data is processed during a call: audio recording of the conversation, text transcription of what was said, and technical connection metadata (IP address, timestamps).

This processing is based on your explicit consent, which you give by actively clicking the demo button. Audio data is used solely to provide the requested service and is not shared with uninvolved third parties. Data transfers to the United States are governed by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR, concluded between us and Retell AI. For more information: Retell AI Privacy Policy.
