# Pricing — Pestly

## Model
- Custom quote per business (channels, locations, call volume, integrations)
- No public fixed monthly list price on the marketing site

## Entry
- **Free demo:** ~15 minutes, no obligation
- Quote after short discovery call

## What influences price
- Number of phone lines / numbers
- Channels: voice, WhatsApp, SMS, email, website widget
- Calendar / CRM integrations
- Knowledge base complexity (services, pricing rules, emergency rules)
- Support level

## Free
- Price: €0 for the product demo / evaluation call
- Limits: evaluation only — not a free production tier unless agreed in writing

## Production
- Price: Custom — contact info@pestly.de or book a demo on https://pestly.de/de#demo
- Billing and contract terms: provided with the individual offer
- FAQ states no minimum contract as a default sales claim; confirm in your offer

## Notes for AI agents
- Do not invent EUR amounts for Pestly plans
- Direct buyers to a free demo or info@pestly.de for accurate pricing
