FAQ

Your questions, answered directly

Is there a free trial? +
Yes. After setup, you have 14 days to test Pestly in your real day-to-day — and see how many requests it converts for you. If you aren't satisfied, our 30-day money-back guarantee on the setup applies. No risk.
Do customers realize they are talking to an AI? +
Pestly learns your personal style and local terminology. Customers usually don't notice. What's much more important to them: They get a qualified response in seconds — while competitors call back hours later.
Do I need to be tech savvy? +
No. We handle 100% of the work. Send us your price list and calendar link — we program, test, and launch your AI within 48 hours. You don't even need to understand how it works. Just keep doing what you do best.
Can the AI understand dialects or unclear speech on the phone? +
Yes. The AI uses state-of-the-art speech recognition that reliably filters and understands regional speech patterns as well as loud background noise (e.g., from a construction site).
What happens if a customer wants to cancel or reschedule an appointment? +
The AI can identify existing appointments in your calendar, cancel or reschedule them at the customer's request, and notify you immediately via push notification.
Do I need to change my existing phone number? +
No. You simply set up a standard call forwarding rule for busy or unanswered calls (after 15 seconds) to the Pestly number. You keep your existing number.
